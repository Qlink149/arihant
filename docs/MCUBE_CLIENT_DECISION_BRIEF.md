## 1. Why this note exists

WhatsApp is already visible in your CRM. Leads, stages, follow-ups, and ownership already run there.

**Phone is still missing.**

For most homebuyers, the call is still the highest-trust moment — the site visit confirmation, the objection, the close. If those conversations live only on personal phones or in a separate dialer, the CRM is incomplete. Managers cannot see effort. Agents lose context. Missed calls die quietly.

This brief is not a technical proposal. It is a **product decision**:

> What should happen in Arihant CRM when someone calls in, when an agent dials out, and when a call is missed or recorded?

Once you tick the choices at the end, we build exactly that — nothing more, nothing less.

---

## 2. What MCUBE is (in one minute)

**MCUBE** is your calling platform — business numbers, agent lines, dialing, and often call recordings.

**Integration** means those calls stop living only inside MCUBE. They become:

- visible on the right customer’s journey in the CRM  
- searchable for managers  
- actionable for agents (alerts, call button, history)  

MCUBE stays the phone system. Arihant CRM becomes the **system of record for the sales conversation** — including voice.

---

## 3. What “world-class” looks like (vision)

A premium CRM does not treat calling as an afterthought. Sales teams that run at that level expect:

| Outcome | What it feels like day-to-day |
|---------|-------------------------------|
| Every meaningful call sits on the customer | Open a lead → see calls next to WhatsApp and notes |
| Missed calls never vanish | Assignee (or a manager) is nudged while the lead is still live |
| Managers can audit without floor-walking | Who called whom, connect rate, who is recovering misses |
| Agents dial from the lead | Fewer wrong numbers, fewer “which lead was that?” moments |
| Recordings support coaching and clarity | Listen back when a promise or complaint is disputed |
| Process rules are intentional | Calling can enforce discipline later — only when *you* define the rules |

Sections 4–6 turn this vision into a **V1 package** you can approve now, and a **full menu** for what comes next.

## 4. Recommended V1 — “Foundation”

### The principle

**V1 enriches the customer story and alerts humans.**  
It does **not** silently rewrite your sales stages or invent new follow-up rules.

That protects the discipline you already trust in the CRM. Automation tied to calling is powerful — and dangerous if guessed. We put it on the menu (Section 5) for you to choose later.

### What’s in Foundation

| Capability | What it means for your team | In V1? |
|------------|-----------------------------|--------|
| **Inbound call logging** | When a customer calls your business number, the CRM tries to find their lead and logs the call | Yes |
| **Outbound click-to-call** | From a lead, agent clicks **Call** → agent phone and customer phone connect through MCUBE | Yes |
| **Call history on the lead** | Direction (in/out), answered or missed, duration, agent, time — on the customer timeline | Yes |
| **Missed-call alert** | If a live lead is missed, the assigned agent gets an in-CRM nudge | Yes |
| **Unmatched / unclear callers** | Unknown numbers or “same phone, many leads” go to a review queue — **no spam auto-leads** | Yes |
| **Recordings when MCUBE provides them** | Call record holds the recording so the team can listen from the CRM | Yes |
| **Auto stage change from a call** | e.g. “Answered → Contacted” without a human | **No — you decide later** |
| **Auto tasks / process rules from a call** | System creates follow-ups or moves pipeline from call events alone | **No — you decide later** |

### What we need from you for V1 (business inputs only)

| Need | Why |
|------|-----|
| Confirm MCUBE is the calling vendor and who owns the MCUBE admin login | So setup and support have a clear owner |
| Spreadsheet: each agent → their MCUBE number | Required for click-to-call and “which agent took this call?” |
| Should **work / secondary numbers** count when matching an inbound caller? | Improves match rate; slightly more chance of ambiguity |
| If one phone matches **more than one lead** — notify and ask a human, or force a pick before logging? | Keeps customer history clean |
| Who should see **unmatched** call alerts? (e.g. managers, admins) | So unknown callers don’t sit unnoticed |

Approve Foundation → we build it. Defer stage/task automation until the decision sheet below says so.

## 7. Clara recommendation (honest)

1. **Approve Foundation (Section 4) first.** You get visibility, click-to-call, history, missed-call alerts, and a clean unmatched queue — without gambling on stage automation.  
2. **Treat process rules (Section 5C) as a short workshop**, not a guess in the first build. Wrong auto-moves destroy trust in the CRM faster than missing a feature.  
3. **Treat phone quality and the unmatched queue as part of “world class,”** not optional polish. Matching the right customer is half the product.  
4. **Pick three “Next” items** from the menu so the roadmap stays sharp after go-live.

We will not invent stage moves or task rules from calling until this sheet says so. That is how we keep Arihant CRM premium — powerful, and under your control.
